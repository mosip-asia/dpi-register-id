# A4 — Sixteen-field identity

## Previous

The identity body was UIN, full name, email, and a face image, plus fields only the mock understood. Gender and date of birth were collected on the no-camera screen and were not on `EnrollRequest`. There was no contract test against the Kit field list.

## Expected

Identity create sends the 16 fields pinned for the Kit: `IDSchemaVersion`, `UIN`, `fullName`, `dateOfBirth`, `gender`, `phone`, `email`, `addressLine1`, `addressLine2`, `addressLine3`, `region`, `province`, `city`, `zone`, `postalCode`, `referenceIdentityNumber`. Dates are `yyyy/MM/dd`. A contract test checks that list. `contracts.json` is not in this repo.

## Now

`build_identity` always emits those 16 fields. `IDSchemaVersion` is 1.1. The face image is a sibling of `identity`, not a field inside it. `EnrollRequest` carries gender, date of birth, phone, and the address fields. The contract test loads `src/register_id/tests/fixtures/identity-required.json` and does not import the expected names from the builder. The in-process mock stores and returns the posted identity fields.

## What was done

`identity_schema.py` holds the field list and the date conversion. The fixture is the contract. Mock storage persists `identity_json` instead of name and email only. `idrepo_client.add_identity` posts `request.identity` from `build_identity`.
