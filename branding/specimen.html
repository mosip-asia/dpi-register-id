Rules

AIT Green is the identifier. Lime is one emphasis on a screen, as a fill or a large heading. Lime, sage, and light grey fail as small text on white.

Use

Keep

  • Green logo on white, white logo on dark photography.
  • Metropolis titles, Lato text, 4 px corners.
  • Campus and landscape photography when a picture is needed.
  • The motto as the institute line, not as button copy.

Leave out

  • A redrawn or recolored globe.
  • Lime or sage as small text on white.
  • Extra brand hues, gradients, and pill buttons.
  • Stock offices and a second product symbol.
